About

I have always had a deep passion for literature, philosophy, and psychology. At UESTC, I pursued dual bachelor's degrees (B.A. and B.S.), specializing in the intersection of NLP and cognitive linguistics.

Large Language Models(LLMs) have fundamentally transformed the landscape of this field, which led me to pursue an M.S. in computer science at Yale University, where I hope to uncover even more exciting discoveries in scalable settings.

My research interests lie in: mechanistic interpretability, test-time scaling and self-evolution, and AI for formal verification.

Human-AI Coexistence

Coexistence between humans and AI is no longer a vision of the future, but our new reality.

I firmly believe that AI Safety remains critically underappreciated -- particularly given that OpenAI's decision to collaborate with the millitary is now well known. If humanity's moral and value judgements lead us to choose war over creation, then AI should somehow transcend us.
